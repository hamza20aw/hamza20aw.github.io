Vision-Based Quadcopter

Custom build · autonomy & computer vision

What

A custom-built quadcopter with an onboard camera for autonomous navigation and visual target detection.

How

4-in-1 ESC with a Pixhawk 2.4.8; OpenCV and YOLO for object detection and QR-code recognition in flight; a custom 6S battery pack built in-house.

Result

Autonomous detection with a stable flight controller–GCS link. The custom pack cut battery cost by 75% and improved time efficiency by 30%.

UVC Disinfection Robot

DC Motor Speed Controller
